Adjunct Lecturer, School of Law, University of Nicosia (2026 – present)

Visiting Lecturer, School of Law, University of Nicosia (2024 – 2026)

I have been teaching on the LLB programme of the University of Nicosia since 2024.

My teaching covers private international law (a module I co-designed and delivered with Dr Nicolas Kyriakides), insolvency law and, from this autumn, banking law.

Teaching materials and student evaluation reports are available on request.

Courses

Banking Law (in Greek), LLB-343

Covers the Cypriot banking framework and associated EU consumer-protection directives.

  • Autumn 2026 (ongoing)

Insolvency Law (in Greek), LLB-358

Ten-week summer intensive course, taught in two three-hour lectures a week.
Covers the Cypriot personal and corporate insolvency regimes, with comparative and EU elements.

  • Summer 2026

Private International Law (in English), LAW-315

Covers EU private international law, with English common-law and comparative US elements.
I delivered five of the module’s ten lectures, covering applicable law, and jurisdiction in family, property and succession matters.

  • Spring 2024
  • Spring 2025
  • Spring 2026
