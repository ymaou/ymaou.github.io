Publications

  • Maou Y and Kyriakides N, ‘Does It Still Fit? Assessing the EU’s Private International Law Framework in the Wake of AI-Related Cross-Border Disputes’ (forthcoming) Journal of Private International Law

    Our article examines whether the EU’s private international law framework, Brussels Ia, remains fit for purpose for cross-border non-contractual disputes arising from the deployment of AI systems.

  • Maou Y and Kyriakides N, ‘Cyprus’ in M Codazzi, P Eichmüller and M Pasqua (eds), Law Applicable to Non-Contractual Obligations Arising out of Privacy and Personality Rights (Edward Elgar, EAPIL Series, forthcoming)

    Our chapter reports on the Cypriot rules on the law applicable to privacy and personality rights, as the national contribution to the EAPIL Young Research Network’s 5th Research Project. For more on this project, see the Research page.

  • Maou Y, ‘Interim Applications and Remedies’ in N Kyriakides and others (eds), Cypriot Civil Procedure (Nomiki Bibliothiki 2025) ch 14

    In my contribution to this commentary, I analysed interim applications and remedies under the new Rules against English CPR practice and case law. For more on this project, see the Research page.

    publisher

  • Maou Y, ‘Judicial Analytics: A Revolutionary Tool of the 21st Century Courtroom’, Procedural Law Unit 3rd Annual Symposium E-Book (University of Nicosia 2024)

    My paper was the first known treatment of judicial analytics in Cypriot legal scholarship. It introduces the application of data analysis and statistical modelling to judicial decisions, and explores the possibilities and conditions of bringing the field into the evaluation of judges in Cyprus.
