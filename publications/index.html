Publications

  • Maou Y and Kyriakides N, ‘Does It Still Fit? Assessing the Suitability of the EU’s Private International Law Framework in the Wake of AI-Related Non-Contractual Cross-Border Disputes’ (forthcoming) Journal of Private International Law

    Examines whether the EU’s private international law framework, Brussels Ia and Rome II, remains fit for purpose for cross-border non-contractual disputes arising from the deployment of AI systems. The article assesses the pressure that AI’s distinctive features place on existing jurisdiction and choice-of-law rules, considers the case for bespoke rules for AI-related harm, and proposes targeted incremental reform of the existing instruments as the pragmatic alternative.

    Presented at the Journal of Private International Law 20th Anniversary Conference, UCL Faculty of Laws, September 2025 event

  • Maou Y and Kyriakides N, ‘Cyprus’ in M Codazzi, P Eichmüller and M Pasqua (eds), Law Applicable to Non-Contractual Obligations Arising out of Privacy and Personality Rights (Edward Elgar, EAPIL Series, forthcoming 2026)

    The Cyprus national report in the EAPIL Young Research Network’s 5th Research Project, a comparative study of twenty-three jurisdictions intended to inform the revision of the Rome II Regulation. The report addresses Cyprus’s position as an EU Member State without a codified private international law act, in which the applicable conflicts rule derives from English common law.

    Presented at the 5th Research Project Conference, University of Luxembourg, March 2026 announcement

  • Maou Y, ‘Interim Applications and Remedies’ in N Kyriakides and others (eds), Cypriot Civil Procedure (Nomiki Bibliothiki 2025) ch 14

    A contribution to the first scholarly commentary on the Cypriot Civil Procedure Rules 2023, written alongside more than twenty Cypriot academics and practitioners. The chapter analyses interim applications and remedies under the new Rules against English CPR practice and case law, including the treatment of freezing injunctions.

    publisher

  • Maou Y, ‘Judicial Analytics: A Revolutionary Tool of the 21st Century Courtroom’, Procedural Law Unit 3rd Annual Symposium E-Book (University of Nicosia 2024)

    The first treatment of judicial analytics in Cypriot legal scholarship. The paper introduces the application of data analysis and statistical modelling to judicial decisions, asks whether and how the field can be brought into judicial evaluation, and proposes a ‘traffic light’ framework that classifies judicial-performance data by reliability, alongside an assessment of the data, language and regulatory preconditions for a Cypriot judicial analytics prototype.

    Presented at the Procedural Law Unit 3rd Annual Symposium, University of Nicosia, December 2023 e-book
