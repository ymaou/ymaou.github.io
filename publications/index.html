Publications

  • Maou Y and Kyriakides N, ‘Does It Still Fit? Assessing the EU’s Private International Law Framework in the Wake of AI-Related Cross-Border Disputes’ (forthcoming) Journal of Private International Law

    Our paper examines whether Brussels Ia, and specifically the jurisdiction-allocating provisions in Articles 7 and 8, remain fit for purpose for cross-border non-contractual disputes arising from the deployment of AI systems. For more on this line of work, see the Research page.

  • Maou Y and Kyriakides N, ‘Cyprus’ in M Codazzi, P Eichmüller and M Pasqua (eds), Law Applicable to Non-Contractual Obligations Arising out of Privacy and Personality Rights (Edward Elgar, EAPIL Series, forthcoming)

    Our chapter reports on the Cypriot rules on the law applicable to privacy and personality rights, as the national contribution to the EAPIL Young Research Network’s 5th Research Project. For more on this project, see the Research page.

  • Maou Y, ‘Interim Applications and Remedies’ in N Kyriakides and others (eds), Cypriot Civil Procedure (Nomiki Bibliothiki 2025) ch 14

    I wrote the freezing injunctions section of chapter 14 in the first scholarly commentary on the Cypriot Civil Procedure Rules 2023. For more on this project, see the Research page.

    Book available from Nomiki Bibliothiki.

  • Maou Y, ‘Judicial Analytics: A Revolutionary Tool of the 21st Century Courtroom’ in The Court of the 21st Century: Personnel and Equipment: Minutes of the 3rd Annual Symposium of the Procedural Law Unit (Hippasus Law Publishing 2024) 40

    My contribution introduces judicial analytics to the Cypriot legal community as a potential assistive performance indicator in judicial evaluations, drawing on the comparative practices of the United States and Australia.

    E-book available from the University of Nicosia.
